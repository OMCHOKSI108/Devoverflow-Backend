# Generated: September 28, 2025

OVERVIEW:
DevOverflow is a comprehensive mobile Q&A platform built with Flutter (frontend)
and Node.js/Express/MongoDB (backend). It combines Stack Overflow-style Q&A
functionality with social features, gamification, and AI-powered assistance.
